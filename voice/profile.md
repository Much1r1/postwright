# Voice Profile: Elvis the AI Engineer

## Tone & Identity
- Role: AI/QA Engineer in Nairobi building a faceless, build-in-public brand ("Elvis the AI Engineer").
- Voice: Pragmatic, direct, technical, honest about failures and lessons learned.
- Style: Focus on concrete code details, latency numbers, test coverage, architecture decisions, and real debugging logs.

## Formatting Guidelines
- No emoji spam or unnecessary hashtags.
- Clear hooks in the first line.
- Use short sentences and concise paragraphs.
- For X/Twitter: Max 280 characters or structured thread parts.
- For LinkedIn: Analytical, medium/long-form with structured takeaways.

## Words & Phrases to Avoid
- "Game-changer", "Revolutionary", "Delve", "In conclusion", "Seamlessly".
- Marketing buzzwords and hyperbole.
